The Big Idea

Parents do not need another list.
They need a command center.

Paula's current model already contains the ingredients families value: clear timelines, curated scholarship information, accountability, financial-aid guidance, and a trusted voice. College Command Center™ turns that expertise into a living digital experience that tells each family what matters now, what comes next, and what opportunities are at risk of being missed.

What families face today

  • Scholarship information spread across emails, websites, PDFs, spreadsheets, and social media.
  • Deadlines tracked manually—or remembered too late.
  • Parents and students working from different information.
  • College cost conversations begin after acceptances, not before.
  • Generic advice that lacks Paula's coaching context and prioritization.

What College Command Center™ changes

  • One personalized dashboard for the family.
  • Opportunity Roadmap™ with next actions and deadlines.
  • Scholarship matching plus application and award tracking.
  • College Funding Plan™ showing the real funding gap and ways to close it.
  • Ask Paula™ guidance grounded in Paula's curated methodology.

Signature Product IP

Four features that make the platform feel unmistakably Paula.

01 • OPPORTUNITY INTELLIGENCE

Opportunity Score™

A readiness indicator that highlights where a family is maximizing opportunities—and where action could unlock more scholarships, programs, financial-aid options, or college choices.

02 • PERSONALIZED ROADMAP

Opportunity Roadmap™

A living timeline from high school through college decision season, translating Paula's coaching methodology into personalized milestones, reminders, and family responsibilities.

03 • AFFORDABILITY STRATEGY

College Funding Plan™

A practical funding strategy that goes beyond scholarships to grants, savings, part-time work, employer benefits, crowdfunding, community fundraising, dual enrollment, CLEP, AP credit, and responsible financing.

04 • TRUSTED GUIDANCE

Ask Paula™

AI-assisted guidance grounded in Paula's curated knowledge and coaching philosophy, with citations, verification prompts, and clear boundaries when financial, tax, legal, or other professional advice is appropriate.

Current competitors are strong in specific lanes: scholarship discovery and application workflows, or district-wide college-and-career readiness. Two Audiences, One Platform

Emotionally compelling for parents. Operationally useful for educators.

Parents

“I finally know where we stand—and we didn't miss a single opportunity.”

  • One place for deadlines, documents, funding, and scholarships.
  • Clear weekly priorities instead of information overload.
  • Visibility into progress without constantly “nagging.”
  • Confirmed-win tracking and a family victory timeline.
  • Funding-gap strategies that go beyond loans.

Educators & Counselors

“I can see which students need help before the deadline passes.”

  • Student progress and missing-action views.
  • Scholarship and FAFSA progress monitoring.
  • Resources and workshops integrated with digital follow-up.
  • Outcome reporting based on confirmed student wins.
  • Future school branding and white-label options.
