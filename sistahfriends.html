How Ideas Become Experiences

From member suggestion to official event

1. Suggest it
Share an event, restaurant, activity, trip or Circle idea.
2. Community interest
Other members can indicate “I'd Go” or “I'm Interested.”
3. DDE reviews logistics
Designer Destinations & Events evaluates timing, venue, cost and feasibility.
4. It becomes official
The strongest ideas can become official Sistah-Friends experiences.
This gives Sistah-Friends a calendar shaped by the women in the community—not just by an admin deciding what everyone should do.
