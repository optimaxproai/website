More Than a Group

A real sisterhood that gets out and lives.

Sistah-Friends exists to make it easier for women of color to meet, connect, try new things and build friendships that continue beyond a single event.

You do not need to already have a circle to join. Come alone. Bring a friend. Bring two. The purpose is to create a welcoming environment where new relationships can actually form.
