Project Documentation

Goals, actions, requirements, and areas requiring careful review.

This section is intended to help the client, designer, developer, and professional advisors align before production begins.

1. Product Goals

  • Create a warm digital space for structured emotional reflection.
  • Extend a trusted helper's support without pretending the helper is available in real time.
  • Help clients recognize recurring patterns in thoughts, emotions, triggers, and behavior.
  • Make AI insights optional, transparent, and non-diagnostic.
  • Give clients control over who may access or contribute to their experience.

2. Recommended Actions Before Build

  • Confirm target users and helper roles.
  • Define whether the product is wellness-focused, clinically supportive, or intended for licensed practices.
  • Obtain legal/privacy review before storing sensitive client information.
  • Create client consent, helper-access, revocation, retention, and deletion rules.
  • Define crisis/escalation messaging and emergency limitations.
  • Complete technical security architecture before production development.

3. Core Requirements

  • Accessible alternative to all audio content, including transcripts.
  • Clear opt-in for helper connections and AI analysis.
  • Client ability to disconnect a helper and revoke future access.
  • Role-based authorization and separation of client/helper/admin privileges.
  • Secure handling of recordings, reflection history, identifiers, and account data.
  • Clear empty, error, revoked-access, and unavailable-audio states.

4. Viable Features

  • Guided reflection workflow.
  • User-generated voice notes.
  • Client-approved helper recordings.
  • Personal weekly pattern summaries.
  • Optional thought-pattern education.
  • Helper invitations and consent management.
  • Audio transcripts and accessibility controls.
  • Non-clinical journaling and reflection prompts.

5. High-Risk Areas

  • AI interpreting emotionally sensitive or crisis-related statements.
  • Representing AI-generated guidance as therapy or professional clinical advice.
  • Helpers viewing client reflections without explicit permission.
  • Storage or transmission of highly sensitive voice recordings and mental-health-related data.
  • Users believing a therapist/helper is actively monitoring them when they are not.
  • Automated conclusions about a user's mental state, diagnosis, risk, or intent.
  • Inadequate procedures for emergencies, abuse disclosures, self-harm, or harm-to-others content.

6. HIPAA / Privacy / Compliance Questions

  • Will HelperVoice be offered directly to consumers, through licensed clinicians, or through covered healthcare organizations?
  • Will the platform create, receive, maintain, or transmit protected health information on behalf of a HIPAA-covered entity?
  • Will vendors handling authentication, storage, analytics, AI, email, voice, or hosting sign appropriate Business Associate Agreements when required?
  • What data is retained, for how long, and who can delete/export it?
  • Are audio files, transcripts, logs, backups, and analytics treated as sensitive data?
  • What breach response, access logging, encryption, least-privilege, and administrative safeguards will be required?
  • What state privacy, consumer-health-data, or professional licensing rules apply in the launch markets?

Important: This prototype does not determine HIPAA applicability or compliance. Formal legal, privacy, security, and clinical review should occur before launch.

7. AI Safety Requirements

  • Use language such as “may reflect” or “you may want to explore” rather than declaring a diagnosis or fact.
  • Give users the ability to dismiss or disagree with suggested patterns.
  • Avoid language implying surveillance, certainty, or clinical authority.
  • Separate educational insights from licensed professional advice.
  • Design defined responses for high-risk or emergency content.
  • Document model limitations and fallback behavior when confidence is low.

8. Decisions Still Needed

  • Who can qualify as a “helper”?
  • Can one client connect to multiple helpers?
  • Can helpers see full reflections, summaries only, or no reflection content?
  • Should helper audio require one-time relationship consent or individual-message approval?
  • Will Coach Ty be a default guide, optional persona, or branded client-specific coach?
  • Which features belong in MVP versus later phases?
  • Who pays: client, helper, practice, employer, payer, or organization?
